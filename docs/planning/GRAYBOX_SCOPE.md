# First playable build

Route: Plot → Starter Street → Neighborhood → Rich Street → Mansion → Facility.

Steal one package with a proximity prompt. A chaser from its area activates after a short warning. Reach your own plot to secure the package and earn test cash. Being caught, dying, resetting or leaving discards the package and ends that chase. Purchase Speed at your plot, then repeat.

All areas are open immediately. Difficulty comes only from increasing chaser speed. No weight, area locks, final art/UI, monetization or content expansion.

Multiple players have separate plots, inventory and chasers. Package spawn claims are atomic on the server. Chasers target their thief only. Cash and Speed level are server-owned. No client-supplied rewards or prices.

Acceptance: earn, caught, one-package limit, repeated runs, Speed purchase, multiplayer isolation, respawn, disconnect cleanup, save/reload and no accumulating packages/chasers/connections.
